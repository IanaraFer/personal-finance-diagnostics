Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data secure? ▼
Yes, your financial data is completely secure. With our offline demo, no data leaves your device. For the web platform, all data is encrypted and stored securely. We never share your information with third parties.
What file formats are supported? ▼
We support CSV, Excel (.xlsx, .xls), and JSON formats. Your file should contain columns for date, amount, transaction type (income/expense), category, and description.
Do I need to register to use the demo? ▼
No! Our offline demo (demo.html) requires no registration or payment. Simply download it and open it in your browser. It works completely offline.
What's included in beta access? ▼
Beta access (€1.99) gives you unlimited access to our web platform with advanced analytics, persistent data storage, detailed dashboards, and priority support for feedback.
How does the health score work? ▼
Your health score (0-100) is calculated based on your savings rate, income level, expense management, and spending category diversity. It receives a grade (A-D) to help you understand your financial wellness at a glance.
Can I export my analysis results? ▼
Yes, with beta access you can export your analysis results in multiple formats for further analysis or sharing with financial advisors.
